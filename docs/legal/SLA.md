## 1. Availability

We aim for **[99.9]% monthly availability** of call answering, measured by our external monitor
calling the readiness check (`/readyz`) every minute from at least two regions, excluding:
scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance on the status page (max. [4] hours a
month, outside [business hours]); failures of carriers, the public telephone network, or the
customer's own phone line and call forwarding; suspension under the Terms; force majeure.

While the platform is down, calls that cannot reach the assistant are forwarded to the business's
staff number by our telephony fallback, where one is configured.
