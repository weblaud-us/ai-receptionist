# Results and reports

**Results** shows what your assistant did for you over the last 7, 30 or 90 days.

| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Calls that would have been missed | If you forward only missed or after-hours calls: every call it answered. If it answers all calls: the ones that came in while you were closed |
| Bookings made | Appointments it booked by phone or WhatsApp. The value is an estimate from your service prices |
| Calls answered | Real calls it handled (your test calls don't count) |
| Handled without your team | Calls it finished without transferring to you |
| Messages & job requests | What it took down for you to call back |
| Typical response time | How fast it replies once the caller stops talking |
| Questions it couldn't answer | Your to-do list for [Knowledge](knowledge.md) |

**E-mails:** a daily summary in the evening, and a monthly "your assistant this month" report on
the 1st. You can turn either off under **Settings → Notifications**.

**Tell us how it's doing:** the 1–10 score on Results comes straight to us, and we read every comment.
