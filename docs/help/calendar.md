# Connect your calendar

With Google Calendar connected, your assistant won't book times that are busy in your calendar,
and every booking it makes appears there.

1. **Settings → Integrations → Google Calendar → Connect.** Sign in with the Google account that
   owns the calendar.
2. Pick the calendar to use. Busy times refresh every few minutes.
3. Make a test booking with **Test call**, then check that it appears in Google Calendar.

**Good to know**

- Only free/busy times are read from Google. The assistant never reads out your calendar entries.
- Bookings made in the dashboard or by the assistant are written to your calendar. Events you add in
  Google only block the time.
- To stop syncing, press **Disconnect**. Bookings already made stay.
- Not updating? Press **Sync now**. Still stuck? Disconnect and reconnect: Google sometimes revokes
  access after a password change.
