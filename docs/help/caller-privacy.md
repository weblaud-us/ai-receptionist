# When a caller asks about their data

You decide what happens to your callers' data. We process it for you, under the Data Processing
Agreement you accepted in **Legal & privacy**. These tools help you answer requests quickly.

## "What do you have about me?" / "Delete my data"

1. Go to **Legal & privacy → A caller asks for their data**.
2. Enter their phone number and the date the request reached you. You usually have **one month** to
   answer (GDPR). The log shows the due date.
3. **Find** shows what's held: contact record, calls, appointments, messages, chats.
4. **Download their data** gives you a file to send them. Send it securely, for example as a
   password-protected attachment.
5. **Delete their data** removes their details. Calls keep only the date and length, which your
   invoices need, with no transcript, recording or number. Upcoming appointments must be moved or
   cancelled first, unless you confirm their details should be removed too. By default the number
   stays on a do-not-contact list so you never text or call it again, which is what most people want.
6. Both actions are logged with dates. That log is your record that you answered in time.

Before sending anything, check it's really them: call them back on that number, or ask something only
they would know.

## "Stop texting me"

Callers can reply STOP to any text. They then get no more texts from your business, and START turns
texts back on. You can also tick **Do not contact** on a contact yourself.

## Recording

Recording is off unless you switch it on (Pro plan). When it's on, the assistant tells callers at
the start of the call. Some places require everyone on the call to consent to recording, and the
announcement covers that, so don't remove it. Recordings are deleted after the number of days set
under **Assistant**.

## Questions

Contact our privacy team at the address in the dashboard (**Legal & privacy → Sub-processors**). If a
caller contacts us directly, we pass the request to you and help you answer it.
