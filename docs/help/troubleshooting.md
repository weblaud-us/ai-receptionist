# Troubleshooting

## Calls aren't reaching the assistant

1. Call your business number from another phone and let it ring out. If you hear your old voicemail,
   forwarding is off: set it up again ([forwarding](call-forwarding.md)). Carriers sometimes reset
   it after a plan change or a new SIM.
2. Call the assistant's number directly (it's under **Phone numbers**). If it answers, the problem is
   forwarding.
3. Is there a banner on **Plan & billing** (payment problem, paused, minutes used up)? Calls then go
   to your transfer number, or callers are asked to call back. Fix the payment or switch off the hard cap.
4. Still nothing? Check the service status page (link in the dashboard sidebar), then contact support.

## "All our lines are busy"

Your plan allows a set number of calls at once. Extra callers go to your transfer number or are asked
to call back. Lots of calls at once from strange numbers is probably spam: send us the numbers.

## It gave a wrong answer

Open the call under **Calls & messages** and read the transcript. Usually the fix is an answer in
[Knowledge](knowledge.md), a price on Services, or your hours. If it did something it shouldn't have,
tell us: we review it and add a test so it doesn't happen again.

## It won't transfer to me

Transfers only happen during the hours set under **Settings → Transfers**, and only to a number that
doesn't forward back to the assistant. Outside those hours it takes a message instead.

## Callers aren't getting texts

- The caller may have replied STOP to an earlier text. They then get no more texts from you, and the
  contact shows **Do not contact**. They can reply START to receive texts again.
- Some countries require sender registration before texts are delivered. We handle that for our
  numbers; tell us if texts to a particular country never arrive.

## Bookings don't appear in Google Calendar

See [calendar](calendar.md): press Sync now, or disconnect and reconnect.
