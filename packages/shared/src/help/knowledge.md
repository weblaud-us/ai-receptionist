# Teach your assistant (Knowledge)

The assistant only says what's in your settings, services and **Knowledge**. It never makes up
prices or policies. When it doesn't know something, it offers to take a message.

- **Import from your website:** Knowledge → Import. It drafts questions and answers from your pages;
  approve, edit or drop each one.
- **Add an answer:** one question per entry, written the way callers ask it ("Do you take
  walk-ins?"), with a short spoken answer ("Yes, until 4 pm on weekdays.").
- **Unanswered questions:** every question your assistant couldn't answer shows up here and in your
  daily and monthly e-mails. Answer it once and the assistant knows it next time.
- **Prices** belong on **Services**, not in Knowledge, so they're always quoted the same way.
- **Special instructions** (Assistant → custom instructions) are for tone and preferences, like
  "always offer the earliest slot first". They can't override the safety rules, such as always saying
  it's an AI and handing over when asked.
