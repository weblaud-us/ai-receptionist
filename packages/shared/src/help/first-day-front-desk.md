# Your first day: front desk

Your practice's virtual assistant answers the phone when you can't. It books appointments, answers
common questions and takes messages. Your job is the part that needs a person.

## Each morning

1. Open the **Inbox**. Callbacks, leads, emergencies and missed confirmations are all here
   ([how the inbox works](inbox.md)).
2. Assign what you'll handle to yourself, so nobody else calls the same patient.
3. Before you ring someone back, open the call under **Calls & messages** and read the transcript.
   The patient won't have to repeat themselves.
4. Mark each item **Done** once it's handled.

## Through the day

- **Consultations & appointments** shows what the assistant booked, plus your own bookings. Move or
  cancel them there; the diary stays in step.
- **Dentists' day** is each dentist's list for today and tomorrow.
- **Patients** has everyone who's called or booked, with their history.
- **Knowledge** is what the assistant tells callers. If you hear it give a wrong answer, tell your
  practice manager: one fix and it's right on every call.

## Good to know

- Callers always hear that they're talking to a virtual assistant.
- If a caller asks for a person, the assistant transfers the call during the hours your practice has
  set, and takes a message outside them.
- **Help → Take the tour** shows you around again at any time.
