# Your team and their roles

Everyone who uses the dashboard gets their own sign-in. Never share one: each person's role decides
what they can see and change, and changes are logged under their name.

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including the team, plan and billing, and legal and privacy |
| Admin | Day-to-day work, plus settings, treatments and fees, Knowledge and the assistant |
| Staff | Calls and messages, the inbox, appointments and patients |
| Dentist | Only **My day**: their own patients today and tomorrow |

## Invite someone

1. **Team → Invite.** Enter their e-mail and pick a role. Only owners can invite.
2. For a **dentist**, pick the practitioner whose day they'll see.
3. They get an e-mail with a link to set their password. The link works for 7 days. If e-mail isn't
   set up, the dashboard shows the link for you to send yourself.

The first time they sign in, a short tour shows them around. Your plan sets how many people can
sign in; the dashboard tells you if you reach it.

## Change or remove someone

On **Team**, change a person's role from the list, or remove them. Removing someone takes away their
access to this practice straight away.

## Keep sign-ins safe

Ask everyone to turn on two-factor sign-in under **Account**. It takes a minute with an
authenticator app.
