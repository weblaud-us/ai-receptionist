# The inbox

The **Inbox** is everything that needs a person: the things your assistant couldn't finish on the
call. Clear it once or twice a day and nobody waits for a callback.

## What lands here

- **Messages**: a caller asked for a callback or left a message. Urgent ones are marked.
- **Leads**: someone asked about a treatment, such as implants or veneers, and wants to hear back.
- **Emergencies**: a caller described an urgent problem and nobody has taken it yet.
- **Confirmations**: the confirmation call didn't reach a patient about an upcoming appointment.

## Working through it

1. Start with **Overdue**. These have waited longest, and the sidebar shows how many there are.
2. Open an item and read what the caller said. For a call, the transcript is under **Calls & messages**.
3. **Assign** it to yourself, or to whoever should ring back, so two people don't call the same patient.
4. Call them back, then mark it **Done**. Done items leave the inbox; the original message or lead
   keeps a record of who handled it.

**Unassigned** shows what nobody has picked up yet. Many practices give one person the first look
each morning.

Dentists don't see the inbox. Their own page is **My day**.
