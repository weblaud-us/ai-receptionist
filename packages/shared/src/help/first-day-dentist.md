# Your first day: dentists

Your dashboard has one page: **My day**. It lists your patients for today and tomorrow, with what
the practice knows about each one.

## On My day

- Each appointment shows the time, the patient and what it's for.
- Where the patient booked by phone, you'll see what they told the assistant, like "chipped a
  front tooth on Saturday", so you know before they sit down.
- Appointments are marked by where they came from: booked by the assistant, booked in the
  dashboard, or from the practice diary.

You only see your own patients. The front desk handles calls, messages and changes to bookings:
ask them to move an appointment.

## Keep your account safe

Open **Account** (your name, at the bottom of the menu) to change your password and turn on
two-factor sign-in. Patient details are health information, so please do both.

**Help → Take the tour** shows you around again at any time.
