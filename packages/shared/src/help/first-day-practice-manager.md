# Your first day: practice manager

As an admin you look after what the assistant knows and how it behaves, as well as the day-to-day
work the front desk does ([front desk guide](first-day-front-desk.md)).

## What you look after

- **Settings**: opening hours, transfers, and **Which calls reach your assistant?** Hours and
  transfers decide when a caller can be put through to a person.
- **Treatments & fees**: prices live here, so the assistant always quotes them the same way. Don't
  put prices in Knowledge.
- **Knowledge**: answers to the questions callers ask. Questions it couldn't answer show up here;
  answer each one once ([more](knowledge.md)).
- **Assistant**: its voice, greeting and hand-offs.

## After any change, make a test call

**Assistant → Test call** lets you talk to it in your browser. Ask a price, book a check-up and ask
for a person. It takes a minute and catches most mistakes before a patient does.

## Each week

- Clear **Unanswered questions** in Knowledge.
- Look at **Results**: calls it caught, appointments it booked, and how often it handed over
  ([what the numbers mean](results.md)).
- Check the **Inbox** has nothing overdue.

## Getting started list

New practices have a **Getting started** list on the overview. Each step ticks itself off as it's
done. If you hid it, **Help → Getting started checklist** brings it back.
