# Live calls

**Live calls** shows the dialer's calls while they ring and while they're connected.

- **Listen**: you hear the call. The person on the other end doesn't hear you.
- **Take over**: the AI goes quiet and they hear you instead. Allow the microphone when your browser
  asks. Take over whenever someone asks for a person, or the AI is struggling.
- **End the call**: hangs up on them straight away. Use it only if the call has gone wrong, and say
  goodbye first if you've taken over.

Listening and taking over need the caller role or above.

After the call, write up the outcome on the lead: what they said, and the next step. That's what the
AI learns from.
