# Call one business

Use **Call a business** (top of the Sales dialer) or **Call now** on a lead when you want the AI to call
one business now, without setting up a campaign.

## 1. Pick the business

- **A business you already have**: search by name or number and pick it.
- **A new business**: paste its website. The AI reads it and finds the phone number, name, address and
  contact. If the site has no number, type it in. You can also enter just a name and number.
- **No website?** Tick **They don't have a website** and type the number. A Facebook, Instagram or
  similar page counts as no website of their own. The AI knows, so it won't say it looked at their
  website, and it suggests an offer that builds websites if you have one published.
- **Any country**: pick it from the list, or type the number with + and the country code. The list
  remembers the country you used last.

## 2. The AI gets the call ready

It reads their website, fills in only the details that are empty, and suggests which offer and script
fit them best, with one line saying why. This takes up to a minute.

## 3. Check the call

The review shows everything the call will do:

- the business details (the ones taken from their website say so: check them)
- what their website says, and the offer and script, which you can change
- the call type and the caller ID
- the exact words the AI opens with
- every compliance check, in plain words

Change anything you need, save it, and the review is worked out again.

**Approve & call** is your sign-off. It records that you checked this call as shown, and the AI calls
straight away. If anything changed in the meantime, you're asked to check it again.

## When a check fails: override it

Some checks are our own rules, not the law. A sales manager can override them:

- the offer's automated test calls haven't passed yet
- outside business hours, where no law sets the calling hours there (the UK, Bangladesh and most other
  countries). Even overridden, the AI only calls between 8 am and 9 pm their time.
- another single call went to them in the last 4 hours
- a campaign called them in the last day

Tick **Override** next to the check. The review is worked out again, and the button becomes
**Override & call**. Say why you're overriding: your name, the reason and what you overrode are kept
with the call.

Checks that come from the law, or from the business itself, can't be overridden: the legal calling
hours (the US, Canada, Australia and the UAE), Australian public holidays, do-not-call lists, consent,
the kind of number, a "no" to the offer, a callback time they asked for and the daily limits. The
screen tells you which ones are stopping the call. Callers see what a manager could override, but
can't override it themselves.

## 4. During and after the call

Listen in, take over (the AI goes quiet and they hear you) or end the call. When it ends you see the
outcome and the AI's summary. Correct the outcome or add a note if you need to.

**Connecting…** (Starting, on Live calls) means the AI is getting ready and their phone hasn't rung
yet. That takes a few seconds. Ending the call now cancels it: nothing is dialled, and you can call
them again straight away. If the AI hasn't started the call within 2 minutes, it's cancelled the same
way. That usually means the sales agent isn't running, so ask whoever runs the platform.

## Who the AI may call

The rules decide, not the AI:

- **AI cold calls to businesses in any country.** In the US only to business landlines. Elsewhere a
  business's mobile is fine too, but the review marks it: make sure it's the business's number, not
  someone's personal phone.
- **Stricter countries show a warning**: the UK, the EU, Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein, Canada,
  Australia, India and the UAE have stricter laws on AI or automated sales calls. The review says what
  the law there asks for. You can still approve the call, and the approval records that you saw it.
- **Do-not-call registers still apply**: UK numbers need the TPS lists loaded, Australian numbers a
  fresh Do Not Call Register wash (Compliance tab), Canadian numbers the national list.
- **AI calls to anyone who agreed to them** (for example through the website's "get a call" form)
  follow the agreement.

## Limits

- Calls go out 8 am to 6 pm, Monday to Saturday, where the business is, and inside the legal hours.
  Where the weekend is Friday and Saturday (Bangladesh, Saudi Arabia, Egypt and others) that's Sunday
  to Thursday. With no legal hours on file for a country, it's 9 am to 6 pm on working days. A sales
  manager can override business hours (see above), but not the legal ones.
- With no caller ID in their country, the AI calls from one of your numbers elsewhere. They see an
  international call, and fewer people answer those.
- Not within 4 hours of another single call to the same business, or within a day of a campaign's call.
- At most 30 single calls a day across the team, 2 at once, and one at a time for you.

A single call doesn't use up a campaign's attempts. If nobody answers, the lead goes back where it
was. If they ask to be called back, you get a task for that time, and the callback is another single
call about the same offer.

Placing single calls needs the caller role or above ([roles](sales-first-day.md)).
