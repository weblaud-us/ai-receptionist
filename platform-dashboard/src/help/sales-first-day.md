# Your first day: sales team

You'll work in the **Sales dialer**. It places outbound calls with our AI, and holds the CRM for
everyone we might sell to. The full playbook is `docs/SALES_PLAYBOOK.md`.

## First, two-factor sign-in

The dialer won't open without it. Set it up under **Account** with an authenticator app.

## Your role

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Viewer | Look at everything |
| Caller | Plus work leads: the power dialer, notes, tasks, wrap-up and do-not-call |
| Manager | Plus offers, the AI drafts, campaigns, imports and exports |
| Admin | Plus the team and the compliance registries (platform admins) |

## The tabs

- **Campaigns**: who we're calling and when. A campaign can't start until its checklist is green.
- **Leads** and **Pipeline**: everyone we might call, and where each deal is. Open a lead for its
  notes, calls and next step.
- **Live calls**: calls happening now ([listening in](live-calls.md)).
- **Offers**: what the AI says and sells. Managers edit these.
- **Results** and **Reports**: how campaigns are doing.
- **Compliance**: do-not-call lists and consent ([the rules](dialer-compliance.md)).

The search box above the tabs finds any lead by name, number or e-mail.
