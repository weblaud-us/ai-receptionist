# Legal documents

**Legal documents** holds the Terms, Privacy Policy, Subscription policy, the DPA and the rest,
exactly as the website shows them at `/legal/…`.

## Editing

- Write in Markdown on the left; the preview on the right is what the website will show.
- Text in **[square brackets]** is a placeholder. A document can't be published until every one is
  filled in.
- **Save draft** keeps your work without publishing it.

## Publishing

- Publishing puts the new version on the website straight away. Signups from then on accept it.
- Tick **A material change** when the change affects customers' rights or what we do with their
  data. Practices are then asked to accept the new version. Typos and formatting are not material.
- Write a short note of what changed. It's kept with the version, and the history shows every one.

Have a lawyer read any material change before you publish it.
