# Your first day: platform admin

This app runs LaudLine itself: every practice, every number, our own sales dialer and the legal
documents the website shows. You can see and change any practice, so take care.

## Before anything else

1. **Turn on two-factor sign-in** under **Account**. Platform admins can't use the app without it.
2. Read [Open a practice as its owner](open-as-owner.md): it's how you'll help most customers.

## Where things are

- **Businesses**: every practice. Open one for its setup, team, numbers, recent calls and history.
- **New business**: set a practice up from its website ([launching a business](launch-a-business.md)).
- **Numbers**: buy, assign and release phone numbers ([numbers](numbers.md)).
- **Pilots**: the free pilots we run by hand ([pilots](pilots.md)).
- **Sales dialer**: our own outbound calls and CRM ([sales](sales-first-day.md)).
- **Transcript review** and **Compliance**: checking the assistant's calls, and caller data requests.
- **Legal documents**: Terms, Privacy and the rest ([legal documents](legal-documents.md)).

## Everything is logged

Changes you make to a practice, and every time you open one as its owner, go in its **Activity**
history with your name. That's deliberate: customers can ask what we changed and why.

**Help → Take the tour** shows you around again at any time.
