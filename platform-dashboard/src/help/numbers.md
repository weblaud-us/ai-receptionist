# Phone numbers

**Numbers** lists every number we hold and which practice it's assigned to.

## Buying

- Australian, UK and EU numbers need a regulatory bundle approved by Twilio before they can be
  bought. The practice's registered business details (Settings, in their dashboard) go into it.
- Approval can take from hours to a few days. You get an e-mail for each new order, and the business
  page shows its progress.
- Until it's approved, the practice can still try the assistant with a browser test call.

## Assigning and releasing

- **Assign** moves one of our numbers to a practice. Calls to it reach that practice's assistant
  straight away.
- **Release** gives a number back. Only release a number once the practice has turned off forwarding
  to it, or their callers will hear nothing.

## Forwarding

Practices usually keep their own number and forward calls to ours. Their guide is
**Forward your calls to your assistant** under Help → Guides, in the "What practice owners see"
section: it has the codes for Australian and UK carriers.
