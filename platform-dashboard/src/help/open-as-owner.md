# Open a practice as its owner

**Open as owner** (on a business's page) opens the practice's own dashboard in a new tab, signed
in as you with owner access. It's the fastest way to see what they see and fix things with them.

## While you're in

- A banner across the top says you're viewing the practice as a platform admin.
- Every change you make is logged with your name, and opening the practice is logged too.
- No welcome tour starts for you. Help → Take the tour still works if you want to show the owner.

## Good practice

- Say what you're about to change before you change it, ideally while they're on the call.
- Don't accept the data processing agreement or legal documents for them: the owner must do that.
- Don't answer a caller's data request on their behalf. Help them do it under Legal & privacy.
- Close the tab when you're done. **Back to platform** in the sidebar returns you here.
