# Pilots

A pilot is a free trial we run by hand: three to five practices at a time, overflow calls first,
and a 15-minute call with the owner every week. The full playbook is `docs/PILOT_PLAYBOOK.md`.

## Start one

1. Set the practice up first (**New business**), and set its plan to manual so it isn't billed.
2. On **Pilots**, pick the business, its contact, the weekly call, and the start and end dates.
3. Each pilot gets a scorecard by e-mail on Mondays: calls it would have missed, bookings, and what
   we've reviewed.

## During the pilot

- Review its calls under **Transcript review** each week. Fix wrong answers in the practice's
  Knowledge with the owner.
- Note on the pilot whether the owner agreed to a testimonial or case study. Never quote them
  without that tick.

## End it

**End pilot** when the dates are up. Then either move them onto a paid plan, or offboard them
cleanly and say thank you.
