## 3. Billing

- Subscriptions are billed in advance through Stripe, monthly or yearly, on the same date each period.
  Card details are held by Stripe, not by us.
- Invoices and receipts are e-mailed to the billing contact and are available from **Plan & billing** in your dashboard.
- If a payment fails we retry it and tell you. If an invoice stays unpaid for {{PAST_DUE_SUSPEND_DAYS}} days we may pause
  the service. While it is paused, calls are forwarded to your practice's own number when one is set,
  or callers are asked to call back. Callers are never left in silence.

## 5. Price changes

We give you at least 30 days' notice by e-mail before a price change affects your subscription. If
you don't want the new price you can cancel before it takes effect.

## 6. Cancelling

- Cancel at any time from **Plan & billing** in your dashboard. The cancellation takes effect at the end of
  the period you have already paid for, and the assistant keeps answering until then.
- After cancellation we keep your data for {{CANCEL_RETENTION_DAYS}} days, so you can export it or come back, and then delete it.
- Remember to switch call forwarding on your phone line back to your front desk.

## 7. Refunds

Fees already paid are not refunded, except where these terms or the law say otherwise. Nothing in this
policy limits rights you have under the law that applies to your practice, including the Australian
Consumer Law in Australia.
