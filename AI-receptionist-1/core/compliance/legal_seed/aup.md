# Acceptable Use Policy

> **DRAFT for counsel review — not legal advice.** Version `2026-11-19`.

**Last updated:** [date]

This policy is part of the Terms of Service. If you break it we may suspend or end the Service,
immediately where needed to protect callers, carriers, other customers or us.

## You must not use the Service to

1. **Make calls or send messages without the consent the law requires**, including marketing
   calls or texts using an artificial or AI voice without prior express (written) consent, calls to
   numbers on do-not-call registers, or calls outside legal calling hours.
2. **Impersonate** a person, business or authority, or configure the assistant to claim to be human
   or to hide that it is an AI.
3. **Record calls without the notice the law requires**, or disable the recording announcement where
   it is required.
4. Harass, threaten, defraud, or deceive anyone; collect payment card numbers, passwords, or
   government ID numbers by voice.
5. Offer emergency, medical-triage, legal or financial-advice services through the assistant, or
   present it as able to help in an emergency.
6. Process special-category data (health, biometric, etc.) in ways your law doesn't allow, or US
   protected health information (HIPAA).
7. Transfer calls to premium-rate or international numbers to generate traffic or fees ("toll fraud"),
   or otherwise abuse telephone networks.
8. Break the law, infringe anyone's rights, send unlawful content, or try to bypass plan limits,
   access other customers' data, probe or overload the Service, or reverse-engineer it.
9. Resell the Service without a written agreement (for example an agency or reseller agreement).

## What we do

We may investigate suspected breaches, block numbers or destinations, limit traffic, suspend
accounts and cooperate with carriers and authorities. Where possible we tell you first and give you
a chance to fix the problem.

Report abuse to [abuse@domain].
