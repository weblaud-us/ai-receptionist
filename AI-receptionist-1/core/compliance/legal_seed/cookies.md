This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on the LaudLine website and dashboard,

## 1. What we use

| Where | Name or kind | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website | Region choice (local storage) | Remembers whether you chose Australia or the United Kingdom | Until you clear it |
| Website, live demo | Google sign-in (set by Google) | Lets you sign in with Google to try the demo. Only loaded when you reach the demo | Set by Google |
| Dashboard | Sign-in cookie (`httpOnly`) | Keeps you signed in securely | Until you sign out, or it expires |
| Dashboard | Chosen practice (local storage) | Remembers which practice you are working on | Until you clear it |

These are strictly necessary for the website and dashboard to work, so they don't need your consent.

## 2. What we don't use

We don't use advertising cookies, and we don't sell or share browsing data. If we add analytics or
other non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

## 3. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. If you block the sign-in cookie, you
won't be able to stay signed in to the dashboard.
